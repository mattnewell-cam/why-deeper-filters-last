Double the material, about — the life

A filter with just enough layers drops below PF 1,000 soon after its first layer loses its charge. Give it more material, split between more layers and more area so the pressure drop stays about the same, and it lasts far longer. Both filters below get the same dusty air, and the clock speeds up as it runs.
